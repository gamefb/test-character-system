DoubleJump
==========

**Inherits:** :ref:`Component<class_Component>` **<** :ref:`Node<class_Node>` **<** :ref:`Object<class_Object>`

Lets the character jump again while in the air.

.. rst-class:: classref-introduction-group

Description
-----------

Pressing ``jump`` in the air jumps as high as a jump from the floor (the ``jump_velocity`` stat). The air jumps come back on landing. How many there are is the ``air_jumps`` stat, so statuses and items can change it: add 1 for a triple jump, or multiply by 0 to take it away while rooted or slowed.

To grant it with an item instead of on the character, give the item's status a :ref:`StatModifier<class_StatModifier>` on ``air_jumps`` and leave :ref:`air_jumps<class_DoubleJump_property_air_jumps>` at 0 here.
