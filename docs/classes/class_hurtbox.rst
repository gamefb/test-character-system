Hurtbox
=======

**Inherits:** :ref:`Area3D<class_Area3D>` **<** :ref:`CollisionObject3D<class_CollisionObject3D>` **<** :ref:`Node3D<class_Node3D>` **<** :ref:`Node<class_Node>` **<** :ref:`Object<class_Object>`

An optional hit zone, such as a head or armor plate, that passes hits on to its character.

.. rst-class:: classref-introduction-group

Description
-----------

Characters without hurtboxes are hit through their own body collider. :ref:`multiplier<class_Hurtbox_property_multiplier>` scales effects that land here, e.g. 2 for headshots.
